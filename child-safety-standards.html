G@ther — Child Safety Standards

Last updated: April 23, 2026

Our Commitment

G@ther is committed to providing a safe environment for all users. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) content or behavior on our platform.

Age Requirements

G@ther is intended for users aged 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that a user is under 13, we will promptly remove their account and associated data.

Prevention Measures

We take the following steps to prevent child sexual abuse and exploitation on our platform:

  • Users must be at least 17 years of age to use the app.
  • User-generated content in event chats is subject to community guidelines that prohibit harmful, abusive, or exploitative content.
  • We actively monitor for and remove any content that violates our policies.
  • We use industry-standard moderation practices to detect and prevent CSAE content.

Reporting

If you encounter any content or behavior on G@ther that you believe involves child sexual abuse or exploitation, please report it immediately:

  • In-app: Use the reporting feature within the app to flag concerning content or users.
  • Email: Contact us at armaharry@gmail.com with details of the concern.

All reports are taken seriously and investigated promptly. We will report confirmed CSAE content to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement authorities as required by law.

Enforcement

Users found to be engaging in or facilitating child sexual abuse or exploitation will have their accounts immediately terminated, and we will cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.

Compliance

G@ther complies with all applicable child safety laws, including but not limited to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and relevant state and international regulations. We report to regional and national authorities as required.

Contact

For questions about our child safety standards, contact us at armaharry@gmail.com.
